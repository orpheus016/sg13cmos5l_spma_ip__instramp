PGIA on real biosignals

Recorded EEG, EMG and ECG driven through slot9_wrapper (the chip as seen from the Chipalooza harness): schematic netlist and extracted layout (kpex C-only, Magic R+C). 1.2 V supply, VCM = 0.6 V, 1 µA bias, typical corner, 27 °C. Transient simulation, no noise sources.

Scope of this run. Only the ideal input-offset null is simulated (vin_off = −334.7 µV for the schematic, −903.7 µV for the extracted layout), at the typical corner, and only for short windows (EEG and ECG 4 s, EMG 1 s) because of the extracted-layout simulation time (about 20–30 min per run). The unnulled case, corners/temperature and longer recordings are scheduled for a later, more complete run.
